Equity, Combinatorics, and Hand Range Analysis in Texas Hold ’Em

Statology · Advanced Equity Analysis

Equity is your share of the pot based on your probability of winning. Combinatorics is the math that tells you how many ways your opponent can hold any specific combination of cards. Hand range analysis puts both together: given how your opponent has played, what distribution of holdings is most likely, and what does your equity look like against that entire distribution rather than a single assumed hand? This is what separates players who think in hands from players who think in ranges. The article is rigorous and the examples are each worked through in full.

Variance, Bankroll Management, and the Law of Large Numbers in Texas Hold ’Em

Statology · Long-Run Statistics

Variance is why a losing player can win for a week and a winning player can lose for a month. The law of large numbers is why the math eventually reasserts itself. Bankroll management is the bridge: the set of rules that keeps you in the game long enough for the math to do its work. This article covers all three with the precision they deserve. You will learn how to calculate your standard deviation, how to size a bankroll against your game's variance, and what confidence intervals around your win rate mean in practice. If you have ever run bad and wondered whether you were unlucky or simply not as good as you thought, this answers it.
